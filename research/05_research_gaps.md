## 2. Methodological Limitations

### 2.1 Archive-Wide Limitations

| Limitation | Impact | Mitigation |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| **English-language bias** | 61 of 63 sources are English-primary (S56 covers 58 locales but for software localization) | ChatGPT track should seek non-English scriptwriting traditions and multilingual craft principles |
| **GitHub selection bias** | Only publicly hosted GitHub repositories discovered. Private repos, non-GitHub platforms, and non-code-based guidance excluded | ChatGPT track covers YouTube, professional web, and academic channels |
| **AI skill format bias** | Many sources (S01, S02, S09, S24, S05) are Claude/LLM skill files, which may reflect AI-first design rather than universal craft | Cross-validate with traditional craft sources |
| **Recency bias** | Most sources created 2023–2025. Historical scriptwriting knowledge (pre-digital) underrepresented | ChatGPT track should include classic craft books (Syd Field, Robert McKee, William Goldman) |
| **No practitioner validation** | No source includes feedback from working screenwriters, video producers, or podcast creators on the methodology | Flag for future validation phase |
| **Fiction over-representation** | 18 of 42 deep-inspected sources focus on fiction. Nonfiction, educational, and commercial scriptwriting are underrepresented | ChatGPT track should prioritize nonfiction and commercial sources |

### 2.2 Source-Specific Limitations

| Source | Limitation | Impact on findings |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| S05 (Crucible) | Single original author, untested | All Crucible-specific findings (F-210–F-213) are LOW-MEDIUM confidence |
| S58 (Faceless guide) | Unvalidated performance claims | All timing claims require external evidence |
| S44 (LLM Analyzer) | LLM-assisted analysis of LLM text | Circular methodology risk |
| S45 (CLT skill) | Classroom research extrapolated to video | Domain transfer assumptions need validation |
| S14 (Humanizer) | Community-identified patterns, not empirically validated | Anti-pattern list is heuristic, not definitive |
| S56 (Mozilla l10n) | Software localization, not scriptwriting | Limited direct applicability; principles may transfer |

### 4.2 Attribution Requirements

All findings are expressed as extracted principles, not verbatim copies of source text. Each finding carries its source ID(s) for attribution. For sources with explicit licenses:
- MIT sources: Include attribution notice
- CC BY sources: Credit author and source
- CC BY-NC sources: Non-commercial use only
- CC BY-SA sources: Share-alike if redistributing
- Public domain: No restrictions
- No license stated: Principles extracted under fair-use analysis; no verbatim reproduction
